Session templates
Strength

Full-body foundation

35–45 min

Use a weight you can control with clean technique. Stop if you feel pain.

  • Goblet squat — 3 × 8
  • Dumbbell bench press — 3 × 8
  • One-arm dumbbell row — 3 × 10 each
  • Romanian deadlift — 2 × 8
  • Dead bug — 2 × 8 each
Skip this session on game day or when fatigued.
Speed & movement

Quick-feet session

20–25 min
  • Easy warm-up — 5 min
  • Acceleration technique — 4 × 10 m, full rest
  • Lateral shuffle — 3 × 10 m each way
  • Backpedal to controlled turn — 3 each side
  • Cool-down walk — 3–5 min
Only do this when fresh, on a safe surface, and not close to a game.
Recovery

Reset session

10–20 min
  • Easy walk or bike — 5–10 min
  • Gentle hip and ankle mobility
  • Comfortable upper-back mobility
  • Slow breathing — 2 min
